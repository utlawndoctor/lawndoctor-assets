**Lawn Maintainer Program** (LMP/M1)
Our core fertilizer and weed control program: 6 visits, spaced 5 to 7 weeks apart, from
March through November, covering 7 services in total. Each visit is tailored to the season,
from pre-emergent and fertilizer in spring to a winterizing fertilizer in the final round.
On round 3, we combine fertilizer and weed control with grub control in the same visit, so
that visit covers two services and is billed as a double application.

**Aeration**
Aeration helps your lawn develop deeper, stronger roots, absorb water and nutrients more
efficiently, and reduces thatch buildup. We aerate in spring and fall.

**Aeration & Overseed**
Aeration relieves soil compaction so your lawn can better use the water and nutrients it's
given. It helps the lawn:
- Develop a deeper, stronger root system
- Absorb water and nutrients more efficiently
- Reduce thatch buildup and improve drainage

Right after we aerate, we spread seed into the holes we've created, using an 80% Kentucky
Bluegrass / 20% Rye blend. Seed goes down in May for the spring visit and in September or
October for the fall visit.

Watering is critical to a good outcome. We'll give you written instructions on exactly how
to water after service so the seed takes.

**Power Seeding**
Used mainly for bare dirt areas or new-build lawns that don't have grass established yet.
The machine roughs up the soil and drops seed directly in. It takes about a full year to
become a full, thick lawn. Done in May, September, and October.

**Soil Enrichment**
Applied on rounds 3 and 4 of the Lawn Maintainer Program. Improves turf health by boosting
microbial activity and nutrient uptake.

**Hydraguard**
Applied on rounds 3 and 4. Hydraguard helps the soil attract and hold moisture at the roots,
improving turf health and seed germination, and can reduce watering needs by up to 50% for
about 3 months.

**Tree & Shrub Program**
Fertilizing treatments for strong root development and color, insect and disease control for
common Utah pests, and dormant oil applications that target overwintering insects and eggs
before they become active.

**Perimeter Pest Program** (aka Pest Control)
Pest Control is a service with 6 applications per year. Each visit, we create a barrier
around your home to stop pests before they get inside: spraying up 4 feet on the exterior
walls, out 2 feet along the foundation, and treating high-risk entry points like window
wells, doors, and windows. If we spot wasp nests along the eaves, we treat those too, and
knock down any nests we can safely reach. This service is exterior only. We don't enter
the home.

**Mosquito Control Program**
Also known as Yard Armour. Targets shaded foliage, under-deck areas, and other common
mosquito rest spots with a blanket lawn and bush spray.

**Bed Weed Control**
A pre-emergent plus selective herbicide applied to flower beds on the first round, with a
selective herbicide on rounds 2 through 6 to keep beds weed free.

**Off Lawn Weed Control**
Off Lawn Weed Control treats the non-grass areas of your property, including driveways,
sidewalks, curbs, gravel and rock beds, fence lines, and landscaping beds, to keep weeds
from taking over.

This service runs 6 rounds per year. Round 1 includes a pre-emergent (to stop new weeds
before they sprout) plus a non-selective herbicide (to kill existing weeds). Rounds 2
through 6 apply the non-selective herbicide to knock down any new growth.

**Grub Control** (GCC -- free curative redo for customers who already paid for preventive treatment)
This visit treats grubs that are still active despite your earlier preventive Grub Control
application. We apply a targeted insecticide to kill the grubs feeding on your lawn's roots.
Since you already paid for the preventive treatment, this follow-up visit is provided at no
additional charge.

**Grub Curative** (GCN -- new customers paying for curative treatment)
Grub Curative treats an active grub infestation already damaging your lawn's roots. We apply
a targeted insecticide aimed specifically at these root-feeding insects. Grubs can be
stubborn, and it doesn't always take just one visit to fully clear an infestation. The price
quoted covers this one application; if grubs are still active afterward, a follow-up
treatment is available at an additional cost.

**Overseeding**
Overseed introduces new grass seed to thicken your lawn, fill in bare or thin spots, and improve
its overall density and color. We use an 80% Kentucky Bluegrass / 20% Rye blend, broadcast
directly onto your existing lawn.

Because this service doesn't include aeration, the seed relies on good soil-to-seed contact from
watering rather than being worked into holes in the soil. Watering is critical to a good outcome.
We'll give you written instructions on exactly how to water after service so the seed takes.

**Sprinkler Blow Out**
This service clears every line in your irrigation system before winter to protect it from freeze damage. We shut down and drain the main water supply, clear each zone individually with a professional-grade air compressor, closest to farthest, run each zone in short cycles to fully purge water without overheating components or over-pressurizing pipes and heads, and drain the backflow preventer. We calibrate air pressure to your system's pipe material and zone layout, enough to fully clear the lines without the cracked pipes and blown-out heads a rushed, one-size-fits-all blowout can cause. Done once each fall, before winter arrives.

**Weed Control Only**
Herbicide treatment focused specifically on weeds, without the full fertilizer program.

**Weed Control (Lawn)** (WCO)
Weed Control treats the weeds already growing in your lawn without harming the grass itself. We apply a selective herbicide formulated to target weeds while leaving your turf unaffected. This is a one-time application, not an ongoing round.

**Fertilizer Only**
Granular, timed-release fertilizer application without weed control.

**Service Call** (SCP/SCS -- typically a $0 follow-up visit, not a paid program)
We stopped by to check on your lawn and take care of anything needed, at no additional charge.

**Spot Seed** (SSF)
We overseeded specific bare or thin spots in your lawn with our Kentucky Bluegrass/Rye blend
to help them fill back in.
